About us

Two brothers,
one animation studio

Animakers is a 3D animation studio founded in 2023 out of a deep love for animation, visual storytelling, and the desire to create something truly awesome. Behind the studio are us — brothers Bagrat and Vahan Maysuryan.

Over 10 years in the industry: we've gone from artists and animators to art directors, working on a wide range of projects. Founding the studio was the natural next step — a way to bring our own ideas to life within a team of like-minded creatives.

Yes, we're a young studio — and that's our strength. We're not afraid to experiment, we seek out fresh approaches, and we love unconventional solutions.

We believe animation is not just movement.
It's emotion. It's story. It's the kind of magic that stays with the viewer.
